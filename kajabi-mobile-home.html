Troubleshooting

Can't see the Community or Courses tabs?

Here are some common solutions:

  • Check Your Enrollment: Make sure you're enrolled in a Sengo membership plan that includes community access.
  • Refresh the App: Close the app completely and reopen it. This can help load new tabs.
  • Update the App: Check your app store for any available updates to the Sengo app.
  • Log Out & Back In: Sometimes logging out and logging back in resolves display issues.
  • Clear App Cache: On iOS, try offloading and reinstalling the app. On Android, clear the app cache in settings.

Still having issues?

Contact our support team at hello@bysengo.com and we'll help you get access!
