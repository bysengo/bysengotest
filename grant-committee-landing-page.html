The Sengo × Venture Partners Grant Program is opening the doors to grant making. We're bringing together a community of donors who want a seat at the table—evaluating founders, deploying funding, and measuring the real-world impact of every dollar.

The Process

How It Works

Three steps to becoming a grant maker and deploying capital that creates lasting impact.

1

Join the Committee

Apply to become a grant committee member. We welcome donors of all sizes who share our mission to fund underrepresented founders.

2

Evaluate & Vote

Review founder applications as a group. Hear pitches, ask questions, and collectively decide which ventures receive grant funding.

3

Deploy & Track

Watch your capital go to work. We track and report on six key impact metrics so you see the real outcomes of your funding decisions.

Measuring What Matters

Impact We Track

Every grant is measured against six categories that capture real-world outcomes for funded founders.

💼
Jobs Created

New positions and economic opportunity generated by funded ventures.

📈
Revenue Generated

Top-line growth and commercial traction achieved post-funding.

💰
Follow-On Funding

Additional capital raised after the initial grant, multiplying your impact.

👥
Customer Acquisition

New customers and users gained, validating product-market fit.

💪
Confidence Built

Founder self-efficacy and momentum cultivated through the grant experience.

📰
Press Coverage

Media exposure and visibility earned by funded founders and their ventures.
