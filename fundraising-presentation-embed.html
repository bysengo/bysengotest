Reimagining Fundraising & Investing

When is the Best Time to Have Investors?

  • Traction First: Wait until you have validated product-market fit with real customer data
  • Clear Milestones: Secure funding when you can demonstrate how capital will accelerate growth
  • Runway Planning: Aim to raise 18-24 months before you actually need the capital
  • Market Timing: Consider economic conditions and sector-specific funding cycles

Key Insight: The best time is when you can negotiate from strength, not desperation. Build leverage through customer validation and revenue growth.

How to Pitch to an Investor?

Essential Elements of a Winning Pitch:

  • The Problem: Articulate a clear, urgent pain point in a large market
  • Your Solution: Demonstrate unique value and competitive advantages
  • Traction & Metrics: Show growth, retention, and unit economics
  • The Team: Highlight expertise and why you're uniquely positioned to win

Remember: Investors back people first. Show passion, coachability, and deep market understanding. Know your numbers cold.

The Danger of Having Investors Too Early

  • Dilution Without Value: Raising too early means giving up equity at the lowest valuation
  • Wrong Direction: Premature funding can push you to scale before finding product-market fit
  • Misaligned Expectations: Early investors may pressure for growth trajectories you can't sustain
  • Loss of Control: Board composition and decision-making power shift away from founders

Warning: Bootstrap as long as possible. Early-stage capital is the most expensive money you'll ever raise. Focus on customer funding first.

Can European Startups Receive U.S. Funding?

Absolutely! Here's How:

  • Delaware C-Corp: Most U.S. investors prefer investing in Delaware entities for legal clarity
  • Flip Structure: Create a U.S. parent company that owns your European subsidiary
  • Network Access: Leverage accelerators, warm introductions, and platforms like Sengo for connections
  • Market Expansion: Show clear plans for U.S. market entry to attract American capital
