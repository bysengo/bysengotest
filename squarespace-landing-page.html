My Full Story

I grew up in the San Fernando Valley in a hardworking, mixed-culture family where discipline, humor, and resilience were part of daily life. Sports shaped me from the beginning — football especially — and I earned championships and All-Conference honors at Crespi Carmelite High School, Moorpark College, and Chico State University.

But my journey wasn't perfect. I made major mistakes in my own recruiting process — choosing schools for the wrong reasons and learning the hard way about fit, culture, and preparation. That experience became the foundation of my life's mission.

I played with and against elite talent — future NFL athletes, national standouts, and some of the best competitors Southern California ever produced. Even with talent, I struggled in recruiting. Not because I wasn't good enough, but because I didn't have the guidance, the mentorship, or the roadmap.

That taught me the most important lesson in recruiting: Talent matters. Clarity matters more. And fit matters most.

For 15 years I've worked in college football recruiting, including 13 years with NCSA, guiding tens of thousands of athletes and families across the country. I've seen what works, what doesn't, and why so many talented athletes fall through the cracks.

I've worn many hats — investigator, bail bondsman, bartender, business owner — all of which sharpened my ability to read people, communicate clearly, and navigate high-pressure situations. But nothing compares to the last 15 years of helping families.

I founded Soul Body Sports because I knew young athletes deserved more. More clarity. More honesty. More mentorship. More personalized coaching.

When you work with Soul Body Sports — you work with me.
