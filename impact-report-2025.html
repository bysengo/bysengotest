Executive Summary

In 2025, Sengo officially launched a new model for how capital moves—one rooted in community, education, and access. Our proof of concept efforts through 2024 consisted of research to understand how funding gaps were exacerbated. We gathered hundreds in our primary markets of New York, Los Angeles, and Dallas.

In January of 2025 we officially received our first grant partner allowing us to roll out our $30,000 grant fund and introduce our membership platform. Through education, events, grants, and fundraising support, Sengo helped founders and funders better understand private markets and participate in them more equitably.

This year marked a transition from early experimentation to a repeatable, scalable ecosystem with measurable outcomes.

Organizational Overview

Sengo exists to democratize access to funding and investing by turning communities into capital engines. Traditional capital systems often exclude founders and everyday individuals from participating meaningfully in private markets. Sengo addresses this gap by combining education, third spaces, and compliant fundraising pathways.

Founded by Ila B. Corcoran, Sengo operates at the intersection of fintech, community building, and capital strategy—helping people understand how money actually moves and how to participate in it responsibly.

Learnings & Adjustments

In 2025, Sengo learned that events alone are not sufficient to drive sustained engagement or outcomes. While events create energy and visibility, the strongest results occurred when events were paired with education and clear next steps.

We also learned that too much variety can create distraction. Many community members expressed a preference for reliable, repeatable support within the platform over a wide range of disconnected experiences.

Additionally, we observed that pay-to-play models can unintentionally exclude participants. To address this, Sengo began offering scholarships and giveaways, and 6.4% of grant capital was intentionally deployed to non-members.

In response, Sengo introduced the Fundraise Readiness Pathway, a free, self-guided assessment available to both members and non-members, helping founders understand their readiness and identify gaps without requiring immediate enrollment.

Looking Ahead: 2026

In 2026, Sengo will focus on deepening investor participation, expanding its impact measurement tools, and scaling repeatable programming across new cities and sectors. We're committed to building infrastructure that creates lasting change—not just visibility.

Ecosystem & Partnerships

Sengo collaborated with ecosystem partners including Honeycomb, BBOP Center, GirlMath, Build in Tulsa, and multiple community hosts and brand sponsors. These partnerships extended reach, reduced costs for founders, and strengthened trust across the ecosystem.
