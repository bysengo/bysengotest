Connect to Airtable

Enter your Airtable Personal Access Token to load your base and display it as an interactive dashboard. Once loaded, you can publish a viewable snapshot that anyone can open without a token.

How to create your token:
1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens
2. Click "Create new token"
3. Add scope: data.records:read (required) and optionally schema.bases:read
4. Under "Access", click "Add a base" and select your base
5. Click "Create token" and paste it above
