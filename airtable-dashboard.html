Connect to Airtable

Paste your Personal Access Token and we'll automatically find your bases and tables. No IDs needed.

How to create your token:
1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens
2. Click "Create new token"
3. Add scopes: data.records:read + schema.bases:read
4. Under "Access", click "Add a base" and select your base(s)
5. Click "Create token" and paste it above
