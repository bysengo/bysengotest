Your Journey

4 Week Experience

This is a suggested sample experience — not expected or guaranteed, but one way to engage with the platform

Week 1

Fundability Rubric + Assessment

Understand your capital readiness. Based on results, we suggest targeted education.

Week 2

Engage & Pressure-Test

Founders can participate in office hours and live sessions for support.

Week 3

Selective Exposure

Founders may opt into pitch competitions or Sengo dealflow for curated visibility and feedback.

Week 4

Audit the Core 4

Founders review their readiness across the four areas investors care most about: data, data room, narrative, and community — gaining clarity on next steps, whether pursuing capital now or continuing to build.
