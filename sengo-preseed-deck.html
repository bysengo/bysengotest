Founder

Ila Corcoran

At 13, Ila began searching for homes online to support her family. Real estate and alternative assets became her path forward, leading to $100M+ in transactions by age 24.

Sengo was created to change that—born from lived experience and the belief that wealth, opportunity, and ownership should be shared.

In Japanese, Sengo (戦後) means “postwar”—the era of renewal and rebuilding.
