Mobilization Over Aggregation

The Sprint Grant Advisory Committee represents a groundbreaking partnership between Sengo and Venture Partners to transform how funding decisions are made in the startup ecosystem. By bringing diverse voices into the decision-making process, we're creating a more inclusive model that reflects the communities we serve while maintaining the speed and flexibility founders need to succeed—without excessive bureaucracy or lengthy underwriting processes.
