Expanding Our Sprint Grant Program

The Sprint Grant Advisory Committee represents a groundbreaking partnership between Sengo and Venture Partners to transform how funding decisions are made in the startup ecosystem.

By incorporating new decision-makers into the funding process, we're building a more inclusive and diverse approach to capital allocation that better reflects the communities we serve.

"

We are thrilled to expand our Sprint Grant Program and partner with Venture Partners to incorporate new decision-makers into the funding process. This collaboration allows us to bring more diverse voices to the table when allocating capital.

Ila B Corcoran
Founder, Sengo
