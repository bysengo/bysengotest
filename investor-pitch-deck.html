5 / 15 Research Evidence

The Venture Capital Diversity Gap

A Systematic Market Failure

Despite representing 32% of the U.S. population, Black and Latino founders receive only 4% of venture capital.

Women-founded companies secured just 1-2.3% of VC funding in 2024—the lowest share in five years.

This represents an estimated $400 billion annual funding gap, creating a significant market inefficiency and unrealized economic potential.

Sources: HBCU.VC (2025), Founders Forum Group (2025), Inc. Magazine (2024)

9 / 15 Root Causes

Systemic Barriers in Venture Capital

Who Makes the Decisions?

82.7% of decision-makers at U.S. VC firms with $50M+ AUM are male, while 17.3% are women.

Only 2% of VCs are Latino/Latina at the partner level.

This homogeneity creates structural barriers through:

  • Pattern matching and affinity bias in investment decisions
  • Closed networks limiting diverse founder access to capital
  • Unconscious bias in due diligence and valuation processes

Sources: Founders Forum Group (2025), Carta Equity Report (2024)
