5 / 10 The Challenge

The Wealth Gap is Real

Only 2% of VC funding goes to women founders.
Less than 1% goes to Black founders.

Traditional fundraising and investing pathways are broken. Access to capital and dealflow remains concentrated among a small, homogenous group.

Sengo exists to change this.

6 / 10 The Solution

What is Sengo?

A platform connecting founders and funders through education, dealflow, and community.

  • Education: Angel investing courses, founder prep programs
  • Dealflow: Curated investment opportunities from vetted founders
  • Resources: Tools, templates, and best practices
  • Events: 90+ annual touchpoints including pitch sessions and networking
