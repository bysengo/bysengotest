How We Work
Our Model
Sengo combines education, technology, and non-dilutive capital into one integrated ecosystem.

Founders — Access education, resources, and funding opportunities to prepare to scale.

Funders — Learn how to invest in early-stage companies and discover high-potential deal flow.

Both — Full access to founder and funder resources for those who build and invest simultaneously.

Reflections
Key Learnings
  • 01 Community is currency — authentic relationships drive better outcomes than transactional networking.
  • 02 Education-first approach works — founders who complete our programs raise capital faster and more effectively.
  • 03 Non-dilutive capital matters — combining grants with education creates outsized impact (167X return).
  • 04 Access is the biggest barrier — when we remove structural barriers, underrepresented founders thrive.
  • 05 Both sides need support — funders need education just as much as founders need capital.
