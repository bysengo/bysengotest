What We Learned

01 Readiness beats access
02 Events without follow-through create noise
03 Founders value clarity over volume
04 Pay-to-play limits discovery of strong builders
05 Small capital, deployed intentionally, creates outsized outcomes

→ Fewer, clearer programs
→ Free readiness tools for members & non-members
→ Deeper 1:1 support at critical moments
