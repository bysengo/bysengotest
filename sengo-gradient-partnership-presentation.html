The Problem

There's a $14 trillion wealth gap in America — fed from both sides. 5.5M businesses start each year but fewer than 3% access venture funding. Women and POC receive less than 2% of VC dollars despite founding nearly half of all new businesses. Meanwhile, 49M Americans are eligible to invest in private companies, but fewer than 2% ever do.

The Solution

Sengo is a SaaS platform reimagining fundraising and investing through education, community, and access. We provide capital readiness tools, workshops, community infrastructure, and pathways to creative capital — turning communities into capital engines for founders and funders alike.

The Market

Private markets are projected to exceed $23 trillion by 2027. The ~$10B alternative fundraising market is massively underpenetrated — 5.3M+ businesses that could raise but never access VC, and 18.2M underrepresented Americans who could invest but haven't. Sengo is the bridge.

Impact (First 365 Days)

  • 600+ active members; 9,000+ email subscribers (4X industry avg open rate)
  • $31,250 in grants deployed to underrepresented founders
  • $5M+ raised by Sengo-supported founders — a 167X multiplier
  • $200K+ lifetime revenue; $50K+ ARR in year one
  • 29 events across NY, LA, Dallas, and virtually

CASE STUDY: SATLYT

Rama Afullo received a $3,000 Sengo grant to support early marketing and operations during her product launch. With Sengo's support, Satlyt acquired 10 new customers, expanded partnerships with satellite operators, and went on to raise $3M from Antler, Google, Slauson & Co, and NASA and hire 12 new team members — proving that small, flexible capital paired with community unlocks massive downstream outcomes.
