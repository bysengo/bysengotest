Building a Full Capital Lifecycle Stack for Founders

The Challenge

Founders need better education on creative capital structures, venture pathways, and venture alternatives

Many founders live in the messy middle. They need help navigating the right capital structures for where they actually are, not where the market assumes they should be. Sengo gives founders access to the creative capital types that fit their stage:

●

Non-Dilutive Capital

Grants, awards, competitions, and government programs — capital that doesn't cost equity.

●

Revenue-Based & Flexible

Education on and relationships to providers of revenue-based financing, SAFEs, convertible notes, and community rounds — helping founders understand and access structures designed for their stage.

●

Angel & Catalytic

Angel investors on the Sengo platform, friends & family rounds, catalytic capital, and impact-first funders aligned with mission-driven founders.

What is Sengo?

The community, education, and readiness layer for founders and funders.

  • Education on multiple capital paths (VC, grants, angels, non-dilutive)

  • Investor education and onboarding
  • Dealflow discovery and curation
  • Funder-founder matching
  • Alternative investment pathways
  • Community connection with vetted founders

The Problem

There's a $14 trillion wealth gap in America — fewer than 3% of businesses access venture funding, fewer than 2% of eligible Americans invest in private companies, and women & POC receive less than 2% of VC dollars.

The Solution

Sengo is a SaaS platform that reimagines fundraising and investing through education, community, and access — turning communities into capital engines for founders and funders alike.

The Market

Private markets are projected to exceed $23 trillion by 2027, with 5.3M+ underserved businesses and 18.2M untapped investors — and the INVEST Act is expanding Reg CF limits to accelerate access.

Impact (First 365 Days)

  • 600+ active members; 9,000+ email subscribers (4X industry avg open rate)
  • $31,250 in grants deployed to underrepresented founders
  • $5M+ raised by Sengo-supported founders — a 167X multiplier
  • 29 events across NY, LA, Dallas, and virtually

CASE STUDY: SATLYT

Rama Afullo received a $3,000 Sengo grant to support early marketing and operations during her product launch. With Sengo's support, Satlyt acquired 10 new customers, expanded partnerships with satellite operators, and went on to raise $3M from Antler, Google, Slauson & Co, and NASA and hire 12 new team members.
