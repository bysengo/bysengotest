For Gradient

Where Sengo
Fits In

Sengo supports the majority of founders who are not yet venture-ready — including rejected applicants, early-stage founders, and alumni — without losing them from the ecosystem.

It extends Gradient's education and workshops beyond the room, providing ongoing support before, during, and after program cycles.

Sengo Enables Gradient To:

  • Provide ongoing founder and funder education
  • Deliver capital readiness tools and assessments
  • Expose founders to multiple capital paths (not just VC)
  • Build community and accountability between sessions
  • Retain alumni engagement after demo day
  • Help founders build toward venture intentionally, not prematurely
For Gradient

Where Vettable
Fits In

Once founders are truly venture-bound, Vettable becomes the system of record — the operational backbone for tracking, matching, and reporting.

It gives Gradient real data to support investor conversations, LP reporting, and cohort analysis.

Vettable Enables Gradient To:

  • Assess venture readiness objectively
  • Track cohort fundability and graduation rates
  • Support faster, more aligned investor conversations
  • Report outcomes to LPs and partners with real data
  • Build a defensible, data-driven capital story
