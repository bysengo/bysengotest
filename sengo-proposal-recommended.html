Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a 90-day strategic engagement to plan, build, launch, and execute a $500,000 community crowdfunding campaign for Sengo in partnership with Boston While Black. The engagement is structured in three phases — strategy and foundation, buildout and pre-launch, and active campaign launch and execution — with clear deliverables, milestones, and accountability metrics at each stage.

At the conclusion of the 90-day period, a comprehensive assessment will determine whether and how to extend or expand the scope based on campaign progress toward the $500K goal.

Campaign Pacing & Community Activation

Pre-Launch Activation (Weeks 1–8)

Build anticipation through insider access, early-bird messaging, and ambassador recruitment. Goal: 200+ warm leads and a "Day 1 investor" cohort ready to commit on launch.

Launch Sprint (Weeks 9–10)

High-intensity 72-hour opening push targeting 15–20% of goal. Leverage social proof, media hits, and BWB community events to generate urgency and momentum.

Sustained Push (Weeks 11–13)

Weekly themed pushes: milestone celebrations, founder story series, investor spotlights. Daily pacing reviews with tactical adjustments based on conversion data.

Post-90-Day Handoff

Comprehensive performance report with campaign health assessment, pacing projections, and clear recommendation on whether and how to extend engagement through campaign close.

Cadence, Reporting & Accountability

Weekly Strategy Sync

60-minute standing call with Sengo leadership. Review progress, surface blockers, align on weekly priorities.

Bi-Weekly Dashboard Report

Written report: funds raised, investor pipeline, conversion rates, community engagement metrics, pacing vs. target.

Monthly Executive Summary

High-level strategic memo to leadership and advisors with risk flags and recommended course corrections.

Key Metrics Tracked

Warm leads generated, email open/click rates, page conversion rate, funds vs. pacing target, ambassador activation, total investor count.

Risk Management

Slow Early Traction

Pre-launch warm list ensures Day 1 commitments. If launch falls below 10% in first week, activate contingency outreach to secondary networks and increase paid promotion budget.

Community Fatigue

Varied content cadence (stories, data, events, testimonials) prevents message repetition. Ambassador program distributes outreach load across trusted community voices.

Platform or Compliance Delays

Platform selection and Reg CF review begins Week 1. Parallel-path two platform options to avoid single point of failure on timeline.

Mid-Campaign Stall

Weekly pacing reviews with pre-planned "momentum plays" (milestone events, media pushes, matching challenges) ready to deploy if velocity drops below target.
