Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a 90-day strategic engagement to plan, build, and launch a $500,000 community crowdfunding campaign for Boston While Black. The engagement covers three phases — strategy and foundation, buildout and pre-launch, and campaign launch with early momentum — with clear deliverables and accountability metrics at each stage. The campaign goes live early May, with the first 5–6 weeks of active fundraising occurring within this engagement.

The full $500K raise will likely extend beyond the initial 90-day period. At the conclusion of the engagement, a comprehensive review will assess campaign performance, project pacing to $500K, and determine whether and how to continue the engagement through campaign close.

Risk Management

Slow Early Traction

Pre-launch warm list ensures Day 1 commitments. If launch falls below 10% in first week, activate contingency outreach to secondary networks and increase paid promotion budget.

Community Fatigue

Varied content cadence (stories, data, events, testimonials) prevents message repetition. Ambassador program distributes outreach load across trusted community voices.

Platform or Compliance Delays

Platform selection and Reg CF review begins Week 1. Parallel-path two platform options to avoid single point of failure on timeline.

Mid-Campaign Stall

Weekly pacing reviews with pre-planned "momentum plays" (milestone events, media pushes, matching challenges) ready to deploy if velocity drops below target.

Alternative Compensation Structures

The recommended structure above is my preferred approach. However, I'm open to discussion on any of the following alternatives. An interactive calculator is provided separately where you can model each option in detail.

Option 1: Cash → Equity Swap

Lower the monthly retainer by 30–40% in exchange for a SAFE note or small equity stake (0.25–0.5%). Best if you're cash-constrained but believe in BWB's long-term valuation. Requires legal documentation.

Option 2: Scope / Hours Reduction

Drop from full execution (15 hrs/wk) to strategy + advisory (10 hrs/wk), where BWB's internal team handles day-to-day execution. Meaningfully lowers cost but shifts more operational responsibility to your team.

Option 3: Deferred Payment

Defer 25% of the retainer to post-campaign close, paid from raise proceeds. A 10% premium applies to the deferred amount to compensate for the delayed payment risk. Reduces your cash burn during the campaign.

Option 4: Retainer + Milestones (Recommended)

The structure detailed above. 65% guaranteed retainer + 35% milestone bonuses. No equity dilution, no deferred risk. Clean, aligned, and signals mutual conviction.
