# EU Capital Pathways Navigator 🇪🇺

> **The EU-native complement to your fundraise readiness score**

## What This Tool Does

The EU Capital Pathways Navigator answers the critical question every EU founder faces:

**"Given where I am, where I'm incorporated, and who my investors are — what capital paths are actually viable for me?"**

This is the first tool that respects EU regulatory complexity while providing actionable, personalized guidance.

## Why This Tool Matters

EU founders are overwhelmed by:
- ✗ Country-specific legal structures
- ✗ Investor eligibility differences (US vs EU vs UK)
- ✗ SEIS/EIS confusion and myths
- ✗ Grant vs VC compatibility conflicts
- ✗ When (if ever) to flip to US entity

**No one shows them what they can actually do without breaking their company.**

That's your wedge.

---

## Core Outputs

### 1. 💰 Capital Compatibility Map

**Input variables:**
- Country of incorporation
- Founder residency
- Revenue stage
- Target investor geography (EU / UK / US)
- Desired check size

**Output:** Viability assessment across:
- ✅ Viable
- ⚠️ Viable with changes
- ❌ Not viable

**For these funding sources:**
- EU angels
- UK angels (SEIS/EIS)
- US angels
- US VCs
- EU crowdfunding
- Regulation Crowdfunding (US)
- EU grants / public funding
- Revenue-based financing

### 2. ⚠️ Structure Stress Test

**Answers:** "If I accept money from X, what breaks?"

**Flags issues like:**
- Need for US issuer entity
- Loss of SEIS/EIS eligibility
- IP ownership conflicts
- State aid conflicts (EU grants + VC)
- Tax residency risks
- PFIC penalties for US investors

### 3. 🇬🇧 SEIS/EIS Reality Check

**Provides:**
- Clear yes/no/maybe eligibility verdict
- Specific reasons why
- What you'd need to change
- What you'd lose if you do change

**Prevents expensive, irreversible mistakes.**

### 4. 🎯 Recommended Capital Paths (Ranked Top 3)

Not "all options" — **actionable priorities**:

**Example output:**
1. **EU angels + IR-PME (France)** → Now
2. **EU crowdfunding** → 6 months
3. **US angels via SPV** → Post-€X ARR

**Each path includes:**
- ✓ Pros / ✗ Cons
- Timeline (realistic)
- Cost + complexity score
- Investor expectations
- Typical check size ranges

### 5. 🔄 Restructure Readiness Signal

**Answers:** "Should I form a US entity?"

- ❌ Not yet
- ⚠️ Only if X happens
- ✅ Yes — here's the cleanest way

**Protects founders from premature, expensive mistakes.**

## FAQ

### Q: Is this legal advice?
**A:** No. This is educational guidance. Users should always consult qualified legal/tax professionals before making decisions.

### Q: How accurate is the SEIS/EIS assessment?
**A:** It's based on publicly available HMRC criteria, but edge cases exist. We recommend getting HMRC advance assurance before relying on it.

### Q: Can I white-label this for my accelerator?
**A:** Yes! The code is yours to customize. Update branding, colors, and CTAs as needed.

### Q: Does this work for non-EU founders?
**A:** It's optimized for EU/UK, but the logic can be adapted. US founders won't get much value (they already have clearer paths).

### Q: How often should I update the tool?
**A:** Review annually (especially after UK budget in March). Monitor EU regulatory changes quarterly.

### Q: Can I charge for access?
