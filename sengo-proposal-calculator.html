90-Day Scope of Work

Three phases across 13 weeks — from strategy through campaign launch and early momentum. The full $500K raise extends beyond this engagement; a 90-day review determines the path forward.

Phase 1: Strategy & Foundation

Weeks 1–4
  • Campaign strategy, positioning & messaging framework
  • Investor personas, platform selection & Reg CF compliance
  • Campaign page copywriting & ambassador outreach plan

Phase 2: Buildout & Pre-Launch

Weeks 5–8
  • Campaign page buildout, email sequences & content calendar
  • Ambassador toolkit & referral program
  • Pre-launch warm list activation (target: 200+ leads)

Phase 3: Launch & Early Campaign Momentum

Weeks 9–13 (Campaign goes live early May)
  • Public launch & 72-hour opening push
  • Daily monitoring, community engagement & momentum pushes
  • 90-day review: performance report, pacing projection to $500K & continuation recommendation
