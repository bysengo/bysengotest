Campaign Pacing & Community Activation

A disciplined approach to building momentum before, during, and after launch.

Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–8)

Build anticipation through insider access, early-bird messaging, and ambassador recruitment. Goal: 200+ warm leads and a "Day 1 investor" cohort ready to commit on launch.

Launch Sprint (Weeks 9–10)

High-intensity 72-hour opening push targeting 15–20% of goal. Leverage social proof, media hits, and community events to create FOMO and early momentum.

Sustained Push (Weeks 11–13)

Weekly themed pushes (milestone celebrations, founder story series, investor spotlights). Monitor pacing daily and adjust tactics based on conversion data.

Post-Engagement Review

Comprehensive performance report with campaign health assessment, pacing projections, and recommendation on whether/how to extend engagement through campaign close.

Cadence, Reporting & Accountability

Structured communication to keep all stakeholders aligned and the campaign on track.

Weekly Strategy Sync

60-minute standing meeting with Sengo leadership to review progress, surface blockers, and align on priorities for the coming week.

Bi-Weekly Dashboard Report

Written report covering: funds raised to date, investor pipeline, conversion rates, community engagement metrics, and campaign pacing vs. target.

Monthly Executive Summary

High-level memo to leadership and advisors with strategic assessment, risk flags, and any recommended course corrections.

Key Accountability Metrics

Warm leads generated, email open/click rates, campaign page conversion rate, funds raised vs. pacing target, ambassador activation rate, investor count.

Risk Management

Proactive identification and mitigation of common crowdfunding campaign risks.

  • Slow Early Traction Mitigation: Pre-launch warm list ensures Day 1 commitments. If launch falls below 10% in first week, activate contingency outreach to secondary networks and increase paid promotion.
  • Community Fatigue Mitigation: Varied content cadence (stories, data, events, testimonials) prevents message repetition. Ambassador program distributes outreach load across trusted voices.
  • Platform or Compliance Delays Mitigation: Platform selection and Reg CF review begins Week 1. Parallel-path two platform options to avoid single point of failure.
  • Mid-Campaign Stall Mitigation: Weekly pacing reviews with pre-planned "momentum plays" (milestone events, media pushes, matching challenges) ready to deploy if velocity drops.
