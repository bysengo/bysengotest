The Problem
A $14 Trillion Wealth Gap

For Founders

Less than 0.5% of Black founders received venture capital in Q2 2025. Underrepresented founders face systemic barriers to capital access, often lacking the networks and resources needed to fundraise effectively.

For Investors

Despite 97% of the US population being technically eligible to invest in private assets, less than 2% actually do. Communities are locked out of wealth-building opportunities in private markets.

The gap is fed from both sides: founders who can't access capital, and communities locked out of private investment.
The Solution
Readiness-First Capital Infrastructure
Sengo helps people make investments in private companies, and helps founders package themselves to receive them.

Readiness Technology

Fundraise Readiness Score + Personalized Roadmap to assess preparedness and create clear action plans

Human Support

1:1 advisory at key inflection points, providing expert guidance when founders need it most

Community Layer

Education, events, and peer learning that transforms social capital into financial capital

Grant Programs

Non-dilutive capital to support founder readiness without giving up equity

Other platforms service transactions. Sengo builds economies.
