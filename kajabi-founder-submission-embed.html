This is a submission form for companies that would like to publish their raise to investors on Sengo. Please only submit if you are raising either 1) crowdfunding, 2) angel funding, or 3) venture funding.

If you are raising non-dilutive funding (e.g. Rewards Crowdfunding, or Pre-sales), you may share your opportunity in the Founder Feed. Please do not submit the form.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the review process take?

+

Our team typically reviews submissions within 5-7 business days. You'll receive an email update on the status of your submission.

What happens after I'm approved?

+

Once approved, your business profile will be shared with our investor network. Interested investors may reach out to you directly for initial conversations. If there's significant interest, you'll be invited to pitch to our community.

Is there a cost to submit?

+

Submitting your business for review is completely free. We believe in removing barriers between founders and investors.

What types of businesses do you accept?

+

We welcome startups across various industries and stages. Our investors have diverse interests, so we encourage all founders to apply. Our review process helps match you with the right investors.

Can I update my submission after submitting?

+

Yes! You can reach out to our team to update your submission with new milestones, metrics, or information at any time.
